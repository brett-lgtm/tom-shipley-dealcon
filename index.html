"I don't need probable. I just need possible."

Tom grew up between Cleveland and Orlando. At 19 he left Florida State to serve in Israel, and made it through special forces selection as a medic. Ten thousand tried out. Fourteen finished.

He started his first company in 1999 with $10,000 and an intern. When the dot-com bust drained its cash, he bought a $13 million subsidiary of Boise Cascade with no cash in the bank and exited a year later.

Then he and his partner Drew built Atlantic Coast Brands from an attic in Richmond. Out of cash again, they bought a $15 million supplement business they couldn't afford. Their little $330,000 brand went to $100 million in three years. Over its life the company did $2 billion in sales, and they sold it to private equity in 2021.

Today Tom and Matt Bodnar run DealCon, where seven to nine figure founders learn to buy and sell companies, and a private community of owners who review live deals together every week.
